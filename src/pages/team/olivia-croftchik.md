## Bio
Olivia Croftchik is an undergraduate researcher at the University of Michigan studying Mechanical Engineering with a Minor in Energy Science and Policy. Her work sits at the intersection of technical design and community impact - she is drawn to solutions that are built with people rather than handed to them, and to understanding how engineering decisions effect people's everyday lives. At Michigan, she serves as VP of Hardware for the Michigan Aquaponics Team and Social Chair for BLUElab Metro, where she has worked on community-centered solar projects in Ypsilanti. Outside of the lab, she enjoys hiking, doing nature photography, and going to the beach.
