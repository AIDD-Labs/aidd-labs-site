This dataset accompanies our reconnaissance report on the 2023 Kahramanmaraş, Türkiye earthquake sequence 20 months after the event (find that report <Link doOpenInNewTab to="turkiye-observations-of-recovery"> here </Link>). It documents organizational and building recovery in two collections:

- **Building Recovery and Reconstruction:** nearly 1,000 organized photos of recovery and reconstruction across 11 locations, including Adıyaman, Antakya, Kahramanmaraş, and several rural villages, with a supporting spreadsheet of image details.
- **Organizational Response to Recovery:** notes and the interview protocol used to document how organizations are supporting recovery.
