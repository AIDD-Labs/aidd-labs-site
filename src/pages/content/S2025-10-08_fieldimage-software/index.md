FieldImage is a Python-based command-line tool, led by Eli Knodel, for teams collecting photographs in the field after disasters. It takes simply organized images, extracts their location information from existing metadata, and outputs the images in an organized file structure with a supporting image data table and interactive map.

The tool has two commands:

- `create-directory-sites` starts an interactive program that helps you set up an organized folder structure for uploading photos by date, site, and photographer.
- `ipro-locator` extracts image time and location data, renames images based on their metadata, optionally identifies and removes near-duplicate images and compresses images, and creates an interactive map of the photos.

FieldImage was developed in AIDD Labs at the University of Michigan with support from the Earthquake Engineering Research Institute (EERI). It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux and is released under the BSD 3-Clause License.
